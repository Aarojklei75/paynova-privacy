Common questions

Where is my plan stored?

On your device, and in your own iCloud account if iCloud sync is on (Settings → Privacy & security → iCloud sync). We can't see your plan. See the privacy policy for details.

My plan isn't showing on my other device.

Make sure both devices are signed in to the same Apple Account, iCloud Drive is on, and iCloud sync is on in PayNova on both. Changes can take a minute to arrive.

How do I restore PayNova Pro?

Open Settings → PayNova Pro and tap Restore purchases. Use the same Apple Account you bought it with.

How do I cancel PayNova Pro?

Pro is billed by Apple. On your iPhone, open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions → PayNova. Cancelling keeps Pro until the end of the period you've paid for.

How do I start over?

In PayNova, open Settings → Start over → Erase all data. This erases your plan from the app (and from iCloud, if sync is on) and can't be undone.

Something looks wrong in my numbers.

Send it from Settings → Feedback and pick Problem. Tell us what you expected to see; your app version and iPhone model are included automatically, and nothing from your plan is.
