PayNova Privacy Policy

Effective October 1, 2026

  • Your budget stays on your devices and in your own iCloud account. We can't see it.
  • PayNova has no ads, no analytics SDKs, and no tracking.
  • We don't sell or share your data with anyone.

What PayNova stores, and where

Everything you enter is stored on your device so the app can plan your paychecks. That includes your pay, bills and spending, savings buckets, goals, debts, and check-ins.

  • iCloud sync. If iCloud sync is on (Settings → iCloud sync), your plan is also saved to your own iCloud account so it appears on your other devices. Apple stores this data under its privacy policy. We have no access to it. You can switch to "Local only" at any time.
  • Home Screen widget. The widget shows a summary that the app saves on your device for it. That summary never leaves your device.
  • Background photos. If you choose a photo as a background, PayNova keeps a copy on your device. It only sees the photo you pick, not the rest of your library.

Information that leaves your device

  • Feedback poll (optional). Settings may show a short two-question poll. If you answer and send it, your two Yes/No answers go to a Google Form we use to decide what to build next. Nothing else is sent with them. No name, email, plan data, or device ID is included. Google handles the submission under its privacy policy.
  • Purchases. PayNova Pro is bought through the App Store. Apple processes the payment and tells the app whether you have Pro. We never receive your payment details.
  • Sharing and export. When you share an image of your plan or export a CSV or PDF, it goes only where you send it, using the iOS share sheet.

Sign in with Apple

Signing in is optional and is used only to unlock Pro for people who were given access. PayNova does not request your name or email. It saves the anonymous ID Apple provides on your device and compares it to a list built into the app. No account is created on any server.

Face ID and notifications

If you turn on Face ID, iOS checks your face. PayNova only learns whether the check passed, and never receives biometric data. Payday and goal reminders are scheduled on your device. They aren't sent from a server.

What we don't do

  • No advertising, and no tracking across apps or websites.
  • No third-party analytics or crash-reporting services.
  • No selling, renting, or sharing of your information.

Deleting your data

Settings → Reset all data erases your plan from the app. If iCloud sync is on, it also erases the copy in your iCloud. Deleting the app removes what's stored on your device. You can also delete PayNova's iCloud data in iOS Settings → [your name] → iCloud → Manage Account Storage. Feedback poll answers are anonymous, so we can't link them back to you.

Children

PayNova is not directed at children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect information from them.

Changes

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top. If a change affects what leaves your device, we'll also mention it in the app's release notes.
